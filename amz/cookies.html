Cookie Policy

How this template handles local browser storage and what to update if advertising or analytics technologies are installed.

Last updated: October 2026.

1. Current configuration

This template uses local browser storage only to remember that the visitor has seen the cookie notice. Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, and other tracking pixels are not installed by default.

2. Essential technologies

Strictly necessary technologies may be used for technical operation, security, and preference storage.

3. Advertising and measurement

If tools such as Meta Pixel or analytics services are added, the website owner should disclose which technologies are used, their purposes, and any choice or consent mechanisms required by applicable law.

4. Browser controls

Visitors may also manage cookies and local storage through their browser settings. Removing local data may cause notices to be displayed again.
