How does the Amazon Associates Program work?

A simple, straightforward guide to understanding Amazon's affiliate program, how affiliate links work, and what to consider before joining.

What is the Amazon Associates Program?

The Amazon Associates Program is an affiliate marketing program offered by Amazon. Eligible publishers, creators, websites, and other approved participants may recommend products or services available on Amazon using specially tagged links.

When a transaction meets Amazon's qualification requirements, the associate may earn a commission. A click by itself does not guarantee a commission.

1. Application

Interested applicants can apply through Amazon's official channels. Approval, eligibility, and continued participation are subject to Amazon's current terms and policies.

2. Content creation

Associates may publish content through eligible channels while following the program's requirements. Amazon's current policies emphasize original content that adds meaningful value, such as commentary, analysis, or other original context.

3. Affiliate links

Amazon provides tagged links that identify associate referrals. When affiliate links are used, the commercial relationship should be disclosed clearly and conspicuously near the relevant content or link.

4. Qualifying purchases

Only transactions that meet the Amazon Associates Program rules may generate commission income. Qualification can depend on traffic source, products, timing, attribution, and other conditions established by Amazon.

5. Commissions

Commission rates may vary by product category and current program terms. There is no fixed earning amount and no guarantee that content, clicks, or traffic will result in sales or commissions.

Important if you plan to use paid advertising. Amazon updated its policies in 2026 regarding purchases generated through certain paid or boosted advertising paths. Before running paid traffic, review the current Amazon Associates Operating Agreement, Program Policies, and Commission Income Statement for the market in which you participate.

Does joining mean you work for Amazon?

No. Participation in the Amazon Associates Program does not, by itself, create an employment relationship, salary arrangement, or official representative status with Amazon. Associates participate under the program's own terms and conditions.

Is income guaranteed?

No. Results vary based on many factors, including audience, content quality, relevance, product selection, consumer behavior, attribution rules, and changes to the program.

This website does not make earnings claims, income projections, guaranteed-result statements, or “get rich quick” representations.

Before you apply

  • Read the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement and Program Policies.
  • Review current commission and qualifying-purchase rules.
  • Check whether your website, app, or social channel meets the applicable eligibility criteria.
  • Clearly disclose advertising and affiliate relationships.
  • Do not present participation as an Amazon job, sponsorship, endorsement, or guaranteed income opportunity.
